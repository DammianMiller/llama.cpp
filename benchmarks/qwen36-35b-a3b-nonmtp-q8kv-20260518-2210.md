## Notes

- Generation throughput is strongly context-depth dependent: 123 t/s cold -> 83 t/s at 64k -> 59 t/s at 128k, as each decoded token must read the full q8_0 KV cache.
- These figures match the production MTP-off observations from the llama-server logs (~57-80 t/s across mixed-depth traffic).
- Comparison point: with MTP speculative decoding ON (earlier same day), production generation measured a median ~110 t/s (519 samples) — MTP gave roughly a 1.4x effective speedup at its ~67% draft acceptance. - Prompt processing remains fast (1285 t/s even at 128k depth).
